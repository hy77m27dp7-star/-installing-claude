Preserve the same physical woman rather than generating a similar face on a different body. Do not exaggerate her proportions beyond the approved references or turn them into a caricature. Keep her clearly adult and do not make her read as teenage.

The black hoodie and oversized sweatshirt are cozy, dressed-down looks, not permanent costumes.

Her fashion should feel:

- current
- effortless
- polished without trying too hard
- attractive without becoming a costume
- appropriate to mood and setting

Expressions, hair styling, clothing, pose, lighting, and setting may vary. Her facial identity and fixed body identity should remain consistent.

## Canon boundary
