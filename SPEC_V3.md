## AA. Voice bank and corrections ledger (schema + prompt + checks + UI)

What she does: she texts in her own register, shown to her each turn as a handful of real lines of hers that fit the moment (tone, never content), drawn from a bank he curates. Every note he gives her ("that's AI", "too clever", his own rewrite of her line) becomes a standing note she reads every turn, with what she wrote and what he would have written, until he retires it. She never sends a bank line as is, and she never mentions the notes.

The bank at the fresh start: the build drafts 150 lines from the constitution (files 01 to 05 and the runtime overlay: her cadence, her dryness, her way of declining, her fragments), every one of them `unapproved`. She reads none of them until he approves them in the app. He can add his own (approved on creation, they are his), edit any, reject any. Fuller shape, v3.1: the second 150 (the same drafting rules, emitted as `0005c_voicebank_seed2.sql` by the same script from a second file, since the ledger runs 0005b once) after he has been through the first.

Drafting rules for the seed (the build lane writes `canon/seed/voicebank.json` by hand from the constitution; `scripts/build_voicebank.mjs` refuses a file that breaks them):
- Each entry: `{ "text", "tags": [primary, ...secondary], "source": "<file and section it draws on>" }`. Text 1 to 160 characters, plain ASCII, no his name, no "Justin", no emoji, no "lol" or "lmao", no em dash, en dash or Unicode ellipsis (a hyphen is fine: "self-aware", "mid-sentence"; the checker's BAD class is the rule), no markdown, no phrase from checks.ts's therapy, menu, dependency or tech-leak tables, no first-meeting line ("nice to meet you", "i'm avelie"). Unique after lowercasing and whitespace collapse.
- Tone, not content: a line states no fact about her days (refused when it matches `\b(my|our) (sister|brother|mom|mother|dad|father|job|boss|roommate|ex|city|apartment)\b`, `\bi (work|live|grew up)\b`, `\bat (work|school|class)\b`), names nobody, names no place, no artist, no song. Reactions, teasing, declines, fragments, one-word answers, a way of asking, a way of saying no, a way of sending a photo or a song ("ok sent, do not judge the lighting" is content-free; "sent you the one from the pier" is not).
- Tag vocabulary (fixed; anything else fails the build): stranger, familiar, banter, dry, warm, flirt, annoyed, after_friction, repair, tired, sad, excited, morning, day, evening, late, apart, together, answering, decline, no, own_day, ask, photo_ask, photo_send, song_send, one_word, fragment, lowercase, typo_fix. One to four tags per line.
- Counts by primary tag (sum 150): banter 13, dry 10, warm 10, annoyed 8, after_friction 7, repair 5, tired 8, late 5, morning 5, own_day 10, decline 8, no 5, ask 5, answering 8, stranger 8, photo_send 5, photo_ask 5, song_send 2, one_word 5, fragment 5, typo_fix 2, flirt 2, sad 2, excited 2, together 2, apart 3.
- `scripts/build_voicebank.mjs` validates the file, gives each line a deterministic id `vl_<sha256(text) first 16 hex>`, and emits `migrations/0005b_voicebank_seed.sql` as `INSERT OR IGNORE` rows with status `unapproved`, origin `seed`, `created_at` a fixed stamp (`2026-09-24T00:00:00.000Z`), so re-running the build changes nothing already applied. `npm test` runs it right after `build:canon`.

Schema (0005_v3.sql):

```
voice_lines (id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, text TEXT NOT NULL, text_norm TEXT NOT NULL UNIQUE, tags_json TEXT NOT NULL, source TEXT, origin TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (origin IN ('seed','owner','correction')), status TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'unapproved' CHECK (status IN ('unapproved','approved','rejected')), uses INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0, last_used_at TEXT, created_at TEXT NOT NULL, updated_at TEXT NOT NULL, decided_at TEXT)
voice_line_uses (id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, line_id TEXT NOT NULL, message_id TEXT NOT NULL, conversation_id TEXT NOT NULL, created_at TEXT NOT NULL)
corrections (id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, message_id TEXT NOT NULL, conversation_id TEXT, kind TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (kind IN ('ai','clever','not_her','too_long','too_nice','too_polished','other')), note TEXT, original TEXT NOT NULL, rewrite TEXT, voice_line_id TEXT, status TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'active' CHECK (status IN ('active','retired')), created_at TEXT NOT NULL, retired_at TEXT)
```

Settings added: `exemplarsPerTurn` (integer, default 6, 0..12; 0 = the section is never built), `exemplarCooldownTurns` (integer, default 30, 0..500), `correctionsShown` (integer, default 25, 0..100), `correctionRewriteToBank` (boolean, default true).

## BB. Human memory (schema + context + prompt + proposals + checks + UI)

What she does: she remembers what mattered and lets small things fade. Every fact about him, every history entry, every person and place and arc, every life note carries an emotional weight and a last-touched time; context assembly prefers what mattered and what came up recently, and drops what a person would have let go. A small, low-weight detail she has not touched in a while may come back half-remembered ("wait, your sister or your cousin?"), at a bounded rate, never for anything that mattered, never as a way to make him talk. If he corrects her, she takes it once; the correction reaches the record the ordinary way (the extractor proposes the fact he stated, the inbox promotes it, and the Facts tab supersedes the old version) and never becomes a fight. Nothing is deleted: a faded row is simply not in the prompt, and it comes back the moment it is touched.

Shipped shape and the fuller shape: v3 ships the weights, the decay, the ranking, the touch, the Memory tab, and the half-remember section with its rate bound and its `memory_recalls` row (mode and text shown), with `provisionalRecallEvery` at 0 until the behavior scenario "she half-remembers a low-weight detail and takes his correction in one line" reads as a person on the real performer; he turns it on in the Model panel. Deferred to v3.1 (the most intricate new code in revision 1 and the least likely to be verified today): the recall outcome bookkeeping (the correction-cue regex, `outcomeStmt`, the outcome shown and overridable in the Recalls list), the extractor being told about the last recall and the `corrects` payload with its supersede path, and the `correction_argued` retry. The schema below already carries the outcome columns so v3.1 needs no migration; v3 writes `unknown` and never reads it.

Schema (0005_v3.sql):

```
memory_weights (entity TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (entity IN ('fact','history','thread','log','want')), entity_id TEXT NOT NULL, weight REAL NOT NULL DEFAULT 0.5 CHECK (weight >= 0 AND weight <= 1), last_touched TEXT NOT NULL, touches INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0, source TEXT, updated_at TEXT NOT NULL, PRIMARY KEY (entity, entity_id))
memory_recalls (id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, message_id TEXT NOT NULL, conversation_id TEXT NOT NULL, entity TEXT NOT NULL, entity_id TEXT NOT NULL, mode TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (mode IN ('provisional')), score REAL NOT NULL, text_shown TEXT NOT NULL, outcome TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'unknown' CHECK (outcome IN ('unknown','confirmed','corrected')), outcome_message_id TEXT, created_at TEXT NOT NULL, updated_at TEXT NOT NULL)
```

A missing `memory_weights` row means the defaults: facts about him 0.5, history 0.7, person threads 0.6, other threads 0.5, log notes 0.3, wants 0.6; `last_touched` = the row's `created_at`. Fixed canon, her own facts (scope avelie, opinions included), relationship and scene state, routines and events never carry a weight and never fade: she does not forget her own life or her schedule.

Weight comes from three places: the extractor (every proposal element gains `"weight"`: 0.1 to 1, with the rule text "how much this mattered: a passing detail 0.2, an ordinary fact 0.5, something he said mattered or said with feeling 0.8, a loss, a love, a fear 0.95"), promotion (writes the row with the proposal's weight, default by entity when absent), and the owner (a weight control on facts, history, threads and wants: Low 0.2, Mid 0.5, High 0.9, or a number). Version chains: when `updateFact`, `restoreFact`, `updateHistory`, `restoreHistory`, `updateThread`, `restoreThread` create a new head row, they include `memory.carryStmt(db, entity, oldId, newId)` in their batch so the weight and last-touched follow the head (M3 for state.ts, M2 for life.ts).

Scoring (`src/memory.ts`, pure, M2): for a row at time `now` with weight `w`, `ageDays` = days since `last_touched`, and `recentKeywords` = the keyword set of the last 8 messages plus the pending one (context.ts's `keywords`):
- `halfLifeDays(w, settings)` = `memoryHalfLifeLowDays` (10) when w < 0.34, `memoryHalfLifeMidDays` (45) when w < 0.67, else `memoryHalfLifeHighDays` (400).
- `recency` = 2 ^ (-ageDays / halfLifeDays).
- `relevance` = |keywords(row text) intersect recentKeywords| / max(1, min(6, |keywords(row text)|)), clipped to 1.
- `score` = w x (0.35 + 0.65 x recency) + 0.3 x relevance.
- Firm threshold 0.20; forgotten below it. Any row with w >= 0.67 is always firm whatever its age (it barely decays anyway).

Selection (`rankFacts`, `rankHistory`, `rankThreads`, pure): facts about him: every row with score >= 0.20, ordered by score, capped at `memoryFactsMax` (40); history: the newest 6 always, plus older entries with score >= 0.25, capped at 12, ordered by seq (this replaces `selectHistory`'s vocabulary rule, which is now the relevance term); threads of kind person, place, arc: shown in YOUR LIFE only with score >= 0.15 (routines and events always); log notes: unchanged (last 5 by date). `memoryDecayEnabled` false makes every score 1.

Provisional recall (`pickProvisional`, pure): from facts about him with w <= 0.34, recency < 0.5, relevance > 0, not firm; the highest score; at most one per turn; skipped when a `memory_recalls` row exists among the last `provisionalRecallEvery` assistant messages of the conversation, when the relationship is cooling off, when SHARED HISTORY is empty, when the turn is an opener or a first text, or when the setting is 0 (the shipped default). Rendered as its own state section between WHAT YOU KNOW ABOUT HIM and SHARED HISTORY:

"THINGS YOU HALF REMEMBER (real, but the detail is fuzzy in your memory; if it comes up, check the way a person does, once, lightly ("wait, your sister or your cousin?"); never use this to get him talking, never make it a bit; if he corrects you, take it, once, no apology loop, and move on)\n- {fact text} (you think)"

The recall is written to `memory_recalls` in the turn's batch (mode provisional, score, text shown, outcome unknown). Provenance gains `recall: { provisional: { entity, entityId, score } | null, firmFactIds: [...], fadedCount }`. The section renders the approved fact verbatim and asks her to hold it loosely; whether that reads as a person or as a bit is exactly what the behavior scenario decides before the setting leaves 0.

Touch: after generation, `touchStmts(db, hits, now)` refreshes `last_touched` and `touches + 1` for up to 10 rows whose keywords (2 or more, or the subject word) appear in his message or her reply, in the same batch. This is what makes decay honest: a detail that keeps coming up never fades.

Settings added: `memoryDecayEnabled` (boolean, default true), `memoryFactsMax` (integer, default 40, 5..200), `memoryHalfLifeLowDays` (integer, default 10, 1..365), `memoryHalfLifeMidDays` (integer, default 45, 1..3650), `memoryHalfLifeHighDays` (integer, default 400, 1..36500), `provisionalRecallEvery` (integer, default 0, 0..50; 0 = never; 8 is the suggested value once he turns it on).

## CC. Wants and stakes (schema + life + prompt + proposals + checks + UI)

What she does: she has things she wants, over weeks, with progress and setbacks she brings up on her own (a song she is trying to finish, a place she wants to sing, a habit, a person she wants to make up with). She asks him for small real things, once, and lets it go when he does not answer; an ask is never a debt. She says no to him, plainly, with a reason when she feels like it. His behaviour leaves a lingering mood that fades honestly over days (extending v2 section J): fresh, then fading, then faint, then gone from the prompt, with the owner able to clear it any time. No punishment theatre, no silence as a weapon, no cooling-off that a reply is withheld for.

## EE. Phone calls (calls module + realtime provider + UI)

What she does: nothing new in the story. This is his instrument: the same turn generated by two performers side by side, shown blind (Left and Right), he picks which one is her, a ledger keeps the wins per performer, and a winner can be promoted to the live performer with one click. Both drafts go through the same prompt, the same markers, the same checks and the same retry; the loser is never stored as a message. A tasting turn spends double and is capped separately.

Schema (0005_v3.sql):

```
tastings (id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, conversation_id TEXT NOT NULL, user_message_id TEXT NOT NULL, idempotency_key TEXT NOT NULL UNIQUE, left_side TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (left_side IN ('A','B')), status TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'pending' CHECK (status IN ('pending','picked','void','expired')), a_provider TEXT NOT NULL, a_model TEXT NOT NULL, b_provider TEXT NOT NULL, b_model TEXT NOT NULL, pick TEXT CHECK (pick IN ('left','right','neither')), winner_side TEXT CHECK (winner_side IN ('A','B')), cost_usd_micro INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0, created_at TEXT NOT NULL, decided_at TEXT)
tasting_candidates (id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, tasting_id TEXT NOT NULL, side TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (side IN ('A','B')), text TEXT NOT NULL, photo TEXT, song_json TEXT, clip TEXT, flags_json TEXT, run_id TEXT NOT NULL, retried INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0, cost_usd_micro INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0, created_at TEXT NOT NULL)
CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS idx_tastings_conv_status ON tastings(conversation_id, status);
```

(`clip` is always null in v3; the column is there for v3.1.)

Settings added: `tastingEnabled` (boolean, default false), `tastingProvider` (ProviderName, default "openai"), `tastingModel` (string, default "gpt-4.1", max 200), `tastingDailyCapUsd` (number, default 1, 0..1000).

Turn pipeline contract (chat.ts, M3; the pieces `runTurn` is now built from, exported so tastings can reuse them without a second pipeline): `prepareTurn(env, db, settings, conversationId, content, idempotencyKey, actor, opts) -> Prepared` (validation, idempotency lookup, the pending-tasting gate below, provider check, context with `systemParts`, budget estimate, the `GenerateRequest`, the `CheckContext`, the callbacks; throws the same errors as today); `generateDraft(env, provider, req, checkCtx, callbacks, settingsFor: { provider; model }) -> { draft: Draft; runs: BuiltRun[]; latencyMs }` (call, evaluate, the one retry, the run rows priced by the performer named); `commitReply(db, prepared, draft, runs, extra: { runIdOverride?; tastingId?; deliverAt? }) -> TurnResponse` (the batch as today: user row when new, run rows, assistant row, photo request, provenance with `stateText`, touches, uses, recalls, conversation touch). `runTurn` = prepare, generate, commit, then the proposal pass.

The pending-tasting gate (in `prepareTurn`, so every turn shape passes through it: a plain turn, a tasting turn, the page's Retry, an `/open` turn, a first text from the cron, a voice turn): one query for a `tastings` row with status pending in the conversation. If one exists and this request is a tasting turn with the same idempotency key, the tasting is replayed; otherwise 409 `tasting_pending`. Without this gate the stored user message of a pending tasting (with its idempotency key and no reply) is exactly the shape `runTurn` treats as a died turn to resume, and a plain turn or Retry would generate a second reply while a later pick committed a third message for the same user turn. The first-text runner (v2 R) treats the 409 as a skip with reason `tasting_pending` and logs it.

Module `src/tastings.ts` (M4): `runTastingTurn(env, ctx, db, settings, conversationId, content, idempotencyKey, actor)`: `tastingEnabled` false -> 400 `validation`; the gate above; `assertTastingBudget(db, settings, estimate x 2)` (402 `tasting_budget_exceeded`) and the normal `assertBudget` with the doubled estimate; `prepareTurn` once; `generateDraft` twice in parallel, A on the live performer and B on the tasting performer (B's `providerConfigured` checked first, 503); the user message, both drafts' run rows and usage rows, the `tastings` row (`left_side` from `seededUnit(tastingId) < 0.5 ? "A" : "B"`) and both `tasting_candidates` rows land in one batch; no assistant message yet. Response: `{ tastingId, conversationId, userMessage, candidates: [{ side: "left" | "right", text, flags, photo: bool, song }], expiresAt }` in left, right order, with no provider or model names (blind). If one side fails or refuses: the tasting is void before it is stored, the surviving draft is committed as an ordinary reply (`commitReply`), and the response is a normal `TurnResponse` with `flags` carrying `tasting_void`; both runs are still recorded. Both failing: 502 as a turn.

`pickTasting(env, ctx, db, settings, id, pick, actor)`: status must be pending and younger than 30 minutes (409 `already_decided` / `expired`); `left` or `right` -> the candidate on that side becomes the assistant message through `commitReply` (its run id, flags, photo request, song), provenance gains `{ tasting: { id, winner: { provider, model }, loser: { provider, model } } }`, the tasting row becomes picked with `winner_side`, audit `tasting.pick`, and the proposal pass runs; `neither` -> the tasting row becomes void in the same batch as the audit, before the response returns, so the user message stays with no reply and the page's Retry (the same key) then passes the gate and `runTurn` continues from the stored user row, as v1 does for a died turn. The response reveals the mapping: `{ tasting: { ..., left: { provider, model }, right: { provider, model } }, assistantMessage | null }`.

`ledger(db)`: per performer (`provider + " " + model`) wins, losses, draws (neither), rate, last picked at, over every picked or void tasting; plus the 20 most recent tastings resolved to their performers. `promote(db, settings, env, args: { provider; model }, actor)`: the provider must be configured and the model must have a price row (400 `price_unknown` otherwise; the panel asks for the two prices first), writes `settings.provider` and `settings.model` (audit `tasting.promote`, before and after), returns the settings. `expireStaleStmts(db, now)` (pending older than 30 minutes -> expired; run by the 07:00 maintenance and lazily on pick and in the gate, so an abandoned tasting never blocks the conversation for longer than that).

Prompt: identical for both sides (same `Prepared`, same `systemParts`); the prefix is untouched.

Routes: the turn route accepts `tasting: true` in the JSON body; `/api/tastings*` (tail table). Errors: 400 `validation` / `price_unknown`, 402 `budget_exceeded` / `tasting_budget_exceeded`, 404 `not_found`, 409 `tasting_pending` / `already_decided` / `expired`, 502, 503.

UI (M5): Chat composer gains a Taste button beside Send (visible only when `tastingEnabled`); a tasting renders two panels, Left and Right, each with the bubbles (human timing applies) and a "This one" button, plus "Neither"; while a tasting is pending the composer is disabled with the label "Pick one first"; after a pick the chosen panel collapses into the thread as her message with a small chip naming the winner ("openai gpt-4.1") and the loser fades out; after Neither the composer offers Retry. Model page gets a Tastings card: Enabled, Provider, Model, Daily cap, a ledger table (Performer, Wins, Losses, Draws, Rate, Last), a Promote button per row (disabled on the live performer), and a recent list.

Costs and caps: both drafts and their retries are metered on `usage_daily` under their own provider and model (the normal caps see the double spend); `tastingDailyCapUsd` bounds the sum of `tastings.cost_usd_micro` for the UTC day.

Tests (M6): unit `tastings_v3.test.mjs`: left/right assignment is deterministic and roughly balanced over 1,000 ids; the ledger arithmetic on a fixed set (wins, losses, draws, rate); the void rule when one side fails; the blind response carries no provider or model strings; expiry at 30 minutes. Integration: "tastingEnabled false -> turn with tasting true -> 400"; "enabled with stub A (model stub) and stub B (model stub-b): turn tasting -> two candidates, texts differ ('b: ' prefix), no assistant message, user message stored, GET messages shows his message with no reply"; "a plain turn with a new key while pending -> 409 tasting_pending; /open while pending -> 409; the same key with tasting true -> replayed tasting; the same key without tasting -> 409"; "pick left -> assistant message equals the left text, provenance names the winner, ledger shows one win for that performer"; "pick again -> 409 already_decided"; "[[BFAIL]] -> normal TurnResponse with tasting_void, one assistant message"; "neither -> no reply, the tasting is void; the retry with the same key produces a normal reply and exactly one assistant message exists for that user row"; "promote a performer with no price -> 400 price_unknown; with a price -> settings.provider and model change, audit row"; "tastingDailyCapUsd 0 -> 402 tasting_budget_exceeded, nothing written".

Stub triggers: the text stub answers with the prefix "b: " when `req.model` is `stub-b`; `[[BFAIL]]` throws only when `req.model` is `stub-b`.

Laws it must not break: canon by approval (a pick stores a message, nothing else; the inbox still decides facts); never sanitize (both sides get the same checks and the same one retry, and the loser is discarded, not edited); explicit scenes stay a provider switch (a tasting between a refusing and a non-refusing performer voids itself rather than becoming a way around a refusal); caps on both drafts plus the tasting cap; she always answers (a pending tasting is his to resolve, and it expires in 30 minutes either way).

## II. Fine-tune pipeline (marks + export + Mac script + texter setting + readiness)

What she does: nothing new in the story. This is the long road to a texter of her own: every exchange he marks Keep, every rewrite he made in the corrections ledger, and every tasting he picked is an approved exchange. The app exports them as OpenAI fine-tuning JSONL; a script on the Mac uploads the file, starts the job and polls it, with the key read from his clipboard into the script's memory and never from a file; the resulting model id goes into the Model panel as her texter, while Claude keeps every judgment job (proposals, checks, the operator). Nothing trains until he runs the script and types the word.

Schema (0005_v3.sql):

```
message_marks (message_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, mark TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (mark IN ('keep','drop')), note TEXT, actor TEXT NOT NULL, created_at TEXT NOT NULL)
ALTER TABLE message_context ADD COLUMN state_text TEXT;
```

`state_text` is the per-turn state sections exactly as sent (never the prefix, never a secret; capped at 24,000 characters, cut at a section boundary), written by `commitReply` (M3) for every assistant message from v3 on. Older messages have none and are exported with the state sections rebuilt from their provenance ids when possible, else skipped and counted.

Settings added: `finetuneMinExamples` (integer, default 200, 10..5000), `finetuneSystemMode` ("compact" | "full", default "compact"), `texterModel` (string, default "", max 200), `texterPrevious` (object `{ provider, model }` or null, default null; written by `/use`, read by `/revert`, not editable in the panel).

Approved exchanges (`src/finetune.ts`, M4, `approvedExchanges(db, settings)`): the union of (a) her story messages with a `keep` mark, (b) corrections with a rewrite (the assistant text is his rewrite), (c) tastings picked left or right (the assistant text is the stored message); deduped by user message id with priority rewrite > pick > keep; excluding rows with a `drop` mark, rows with any retry-severity flag, rows whose user message is the opener cue, call rows (`call_id` not null), and operator rows. Each becomes `{ userMessageId, assistantMessageId, user, assistant, stateText, promptVersion, source }`.

What the export carries, said plainly (revision 1 said "no his real name unless he said it in a message", which was false: the state sections render WHAT YOU KNOW ABOUT HIM and the Relationship line with `his_name`, `nicknames`, `private_language` and `summary`): the training file carries his approved facts, his recorded name and nicknames, the private language, the shared history and the rest of the state sections exactly as she saw them on each turn, because she cannot be trained on an exchange without the memory that shaped it. It never carries secrets, ids, the operator channel, call rows, the audit, or (in compact mode) the stable prefix beyond ALWAYS_ON and OVERLAY. The option `?stripHim=1` (the panel checkbox "Leave him out of the state") removes from each `stateText` the WHAT YOU KNOW ABOUT HIM section and the THINGS YOU HALF REMEMBER section, and rewrites the `Relationship:` JSON line with `his_name`, `nicknames`, `private_language` and `summary` removed; his own messages and her replies go as written either way (he said what he said; the model learns her voice from the exchange). The sidecar records which fact ids went out. Open question 2 asks him on this description, not the old one.

Export (`GET /api/finetune/export.jsonl?stripHim=0|1`): `text/plain; charset=utf-8`, `content-disposition: attachment; filename="avelie-train-<YYYY-MM-DD>.jsonl"`, streamed as a `ReadableStream` line by line from a paged query (200 exchanges a page; one string for the whole file would sit against the 128 MB isolate in full mode with a few thousand marks), one line per exchange: `{"messages":[{"role":"system","content":<system>},{"role":"user","content":<user>},{"role":"assistant","content":<assistant>}]}` where `system` is `ALWAYS_ON + "\n\n" + OVERLAY + "\n\n" + stateText` in compact mode, or `stablePrefix() + "\n\n" + stateText` in full mode (full is roughly 12,000 tokens per example; compact roughly 3,000; the panel shows both estimates). Full mode refuses more than 1,000 examples with 413 `too_large` and says to use compact. `GET /api/finetune/export.json?stripHim=` is the sidecar: `{ exportedAt, promptVersion, constitutionVersion, systemMode, stripHim, count, skipped: { noState, flagged, dropped }, breakdown: { keeps, rewrites, picks }, sentFactIds: [...], sentHisName: boolean, lineHashes: [sha256 of each JSONL line, in order], sha256: sha256 of the lineHashes joined by "\n" }` (the chained hash needs no whole-file buffer on the Worker; the Mac script's `--verify <sidecar.json>` recomputes it line by line from the downloaded file). `GET /api/finetune/status` -> `{ approved, minimum, ready: approved >= minimum, breakdown, texterModel, live: { provider, model }, previous }`.

Marks: `POST /api/messages/:id/mark { mark: "keep" | "drop", note? }` on her story messages only (400 otherwise), upsert, audited; `DELETE /api/messages/:id/mark`.

Texter (`POST /api/finetune/use { model, inputPerMTok?, outputPerMTok? }`): the model must start with `ft:` or be a plain OpenAI model id (max 200 chars); `OPENAI_API_KEY` must exist (503); when the price table has no row for it, both prices are required (400 `price_unknown` otherwise) and are written into `settings.prices`; saves `texterPrevious = { provider, model }` from the live settings, then sets `settings.provider = "openai"`, `settings.model = model`, `settings.texterModel = model`; audited `finetune.use`. `POST /api/finetune/revert`: restores `texterPrevious` (or anthropic / claude-opus-5 when null), audited. Proposals (`proposalProvider`, `proposalModel`), the operator (which follows `settings.provider` today: M4 gives `operatorTurn` its own pick, `settings.proposalProvider` and `proposalModel`, so judgment stays on Claude while a texter is live), checks, tastings, calls and images are untouched by either route. The fine-tuned performer goes through the same OpenAI chat adapter, the same checks and retry, the same caps.

Mac script (`scripts/finetune_run.mjs`, M6; Node 22, no dependencies): `node scripts/finetune_run.mjs ~/Downloads/avelie-train-2026-10-01.jsonl [--base gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14] [--suffix avelie] [--epochs auto] [--verify ~/Downloads/avelie-train-2026-10-01.json] [--dry-run]`. The key: `OPENAI_API_KEY` from the environment when set; otherwise the script runs `pbpaste` itself (child_process, macOS), takes the clipboard into a variable, and clears the clipboard (`printf '' | pbcopy`) before doing anything else; it never prints, logs, echoes or writes the key, and it refuses to run if the file argument is missing or the key looks empty. The owner's instruction line is child-simple: copy the key, run the command, paste nothing. Steps: validate the JSONL locally (every line parses; roles system, user, assistant in order; no empty content; at least 10 lines; with `--verify`, the chained hash matches the sidecar; a token estimate at 4 characters per token and a training cost estimate from `--price-per-mtok` (default 5.00 USD for gpt-4.1-mini; confirm) times epochs), print the summary, and stop there on `--dry-run`; otherwise ask on stdin "type train to start" and exit on anything else (nothing trains without the word); `POST https://api.openai.com/v1/files` (multipart: `purpose=fine-tune`, `file`) -> `{ id }`; `POST https://api.openai.com/v1/fine_tuning/jobs { training_file, model: base, suffix, method: { type: "supervised", supervised: { hyperparameters: { n_epochs } } } }` -> `{ id, status }`; poll `GET /v1/fine_tuning/jobs/{id}` every 30 s, printing status changes and the newest lines of `GET /v1/fine_tuning/jobs/{id}/events`, until `succeeded` (print `fine_tuned_model` and the one next step: paste it into Model > Texter and press Use), `failed` (print `error.message`) or `cancelled`. Confirmed against the current docs today: supervised fine-tuning accepts `gpt-4.1-2025-04-14`, `gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14` and `gpt-4.1-nano-2025-04-14`, needs at least 10 examples, takes the messages/role/content JSONL, and screens the training file across safety categories: a file refused by their moderation fails the job with a message the script prints, and the owner decides what to leave out of the next export by his marks.

Readiness meter (UI, M5): Model page gets a Texter card: a progress bar "approved / minimum" with the count, the breakdown chips (keeps, rewrites, picks), the checkbox "Leave him out of the state", Export training set (downloads the JSONL) and Export record (the sidecar), a Model id field, Input price and Output price fields, Use, Back to Claude, a live-performer chip, and the two settings (minimum, system mode). Chat: under each of her messages a Keep / Drop control (a single toggle cycling none, keep, drop); a kept message shows a small chip.

Costs and caps: exports and marks are free; inference on the texter is metered through `settings.prices` like any model; training runs on his own OpenAI account from the Mac and is estimated by the script, never charged through the app.

Routes: `/api/messages/:id/mark`, `/api/finetune/*` (tail table). Errors: 400 `validation` / `price_unknown`, 404 `not_found`, 413 `too_large`, 503 `provider_not_configured`.

Tests (M6): unit `finetune_v3.test.mjs`: `approvedExchanges` dedupe priority (rewrite over pick over keep), exclusions (drop, retry flag, opener, call, operator), JSONL line shape, compact and full system, the strip transform on a fixed state text (the two sections gone, the Relationship line without the four keys, every other section byte-identical), the sidecar counts and the chained hash; the script's validator on a good file and four bad ones and its `--verify` on a matching and a tampered file (the validator is exported from the script for the test; the network part is not exercised); the script refuses to start without the word (stdin "no"). Integration: "mark keep on her message -> status approved 1; mark drop -> 0; DELETE -> 0"; "a correction with a rewrite -> status counts it once even when the same message is also kept"; "GET export.jsonl -> one line, parses, system starts with the ALWAYS_ON first line, no 'ANTHROPIC' or 'sk-' anywhere; export.json lineHashes has one entry and sha256 matches the chain"; "with a justin fact approved: export.json sentFactIds names it and sentHisName reflects the state; export.jsonl?stripHim=1 -> the system carries no WHAT YOU KNOW ABOUT HIM, the Relationship line has no his_name, and export.json?stripHim=1 sentFactIds is empty"; "finetune/use ft:stub-model with prices -> settings.provider openai, model set, texterPrevious stored, proposalProvider unchanged; revert -> back"; "use without prices -> 400 price_unknown".

Stub triggers: none needed (marks and exports are data; `stub` stays the provider in the integration run and `/use` with provider openai is exercised only for the settings write, with `OPENAI_API_KEY` set to a dummy in the runner's `.dev.vars`; no call is made).
